Privacy & Terms

Summary

In short Crimstone only keeps what its features need to work: XP, server settings, moderation records, giveaway entries, open tickets, and things you set yourself like reminders. It never sells your data or uses it for ads. You can ask for any of it to be deleted.

What Crimstone Saves

Levels Your Discord user ID and XP in each server. Your level is worked out from your XP. If a server turns on voice XP, you also earn XP for time spent unmuted in a voice channel with others, but nothing about the call itself is saved.
Server settings Channel, role and feature choices made with /config, /auditlog, /automod, /welcome, /starboard, /ticket, /xpboost, /alerts, /levelrole and /rankcard. This includes a server's blocked-word list, its welcome and leave messages, and the YouTube or Twitch channels it follows.
Moderation Warnings (reason and moderator) and a case history of actions taken through Crimstone, including timeouts and kicks made by automod: action, user, moderator, reason, duration and time. The latest 500 cases per server are kept.
Giveaways The host, prize, end time, and the user IDs of everyone who entered, so winners can be picked and rerolled. Ended giveaways are kept for 7 days, then cleared out the next time the bot restarts.
Tickets While a ticket is open: who opened it, its number and when. This is removed when the ticket is closed.
Starboard Which messages have been reposted to the starboard and the ID of each repost, so star counts can be kept up to date
Your settings Your AFK message until you next talk, your reminders until they are sent or cancelled, and your rank card style (colors, image link, tagline)
Activity Message counts per day and per channel in each server, for /serverstats. No message content and no user IDs. Daily counts are kept for 14 days.

What Crimstone Does Not Save

Messages Message content is read to award XP, answer commands and check automod rules, then discarded. It is not written to disk.
Deleted messages For /snipe, the last 5 deleted messages in each channel are held in memory only, and cleared whenever the bot restarts
Automod To catch spam and raids, automod holds the time and ID of your recent messages for up to a minute, and the times of recent joins (no user IDs) for up to 10 minutes, in memory only
Personal details No email address, real name, IP address, payment details, DMs or voice audio

Logs & Messages In Your Server

Mod & audit logs If a server's admins turn these on, Crimstone posts events such as bans, edited or deleted messages, joins, leaves and voice moves into a channel they choose. When automod removes a message, its content is posted there too. Those posts live in Discord and are controlled by the server.
Ticket transcripts When a ticket is closed, Crimstone builds a transcript of up to 1,000 of its messages and sends it to the person who opened it, and to the server's ticket log channel if one is set. Crimstone does not keep a copy.
Welcome & starboard Welcome and leave messages can name you when you join or leave. Starboard reposts a message's text and image to a highlights channel once it gets enough stars.
Public by design Rank cards, leaderboards, giveaway winners and command replies are visible to anyone who can read the channel. Your XP can appear on the global leaderboard unless your server opts out with /config.
Direct messages Crimstone may DM you when you are warned (with the reason), when your ticket is closed (with its transcript), or when automod removes you from a server because your account is too new to join

Outside Services

When used Some commands send the text you type to an outside service to get an answer. Your Discord account is not sent with it, except for /vote.
Which ones /translate (Google Translate, MyMemory), /weather (wttr.in), /crypto (CoinGecko), /define (Free Dictionary API), /mcskin and /mcstatus (Minotar, mcsrvstat.us, mc-api.net), /github-stars (GitHub), /catfact (catfact.ninja), /alerts (YouTube, Twitch)
top.gg /vote sends your Discord user ID to top.gg to check whether you have voted. Crimstone also shares how many servers it is in with top.gg.
Discord Everything the bot does goes through Discord, which has its own privacy policy

Keeping & Deleting Data

How long Saved data is kept until it is deleted. Removing Crimstone from a server does not erase that server's data automatically, so ask if you want it gone.
Do it yourself Clear your AFK by talking, cancel reminders with /reminder cancel, and reset your rank card with /rankcard reset. Admins can use /config reset, /auditlog reset, /automod reset, /welcome reset, /starboard disable, /alerts remove and /xpboost remove.
Ask Email or message the support server with your Discord user ID and the server it concerns. You can get a copy of your data, have it corrected, or have it deleted, free and within 30 days. Deleted XP cannot be restored.
Your rights You may have extra rights under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act. They are honored wherever you live. Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is legitimate interest: running a bot your server chose to add.

Security & Age

Security Data is stored on a self-hosted machine only the operator can access. No system is perfectly secure, so do not put sensitive information into the bot. If a breach affects your data, it will be announced in the support server.
Age You must be at least 13, or older if your country requires it, as Discord requires. If you think a child's data has been stored, get in touch and it will be deleted.
