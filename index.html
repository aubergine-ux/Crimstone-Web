A free Discord bot for moderation, leveling and more

Crimstone is a 100% free, fully open-source multi-purpose Discord bot. It comes with 75 slash commands for moderation, automod, warnings and case history, leveling with custom rank cards and level roles, audit logs, welcome messages, tickets, giveaways, reminders, and plenty of utility and fun commands to help you run your server effortlessly!

Moderation
